# C3-data150 — Learning curve: 150 training episodes (default 600)

*parent* `C1-where` · *contribution* C3 · *status* done · jobs 18/18

**Question.** How much data does a learned observer need before it pays off?

**Hypothesis.** Error falls roughly as a power law in n_train; hyper needs more data than latent.

**Decision rule.** Report error vs n_train (50/150/600) per method; crossover with the filters under 30% mismatch read from C3-mismatch30.

# Metric `nrmse`

Definitions in hyperkkl/metrics.py. Mean over training seeds with a Student-t 95% CI over seeds (single-seed nodes: episode bootstrap, which ignores training variability); lower is better. Paired cells: mean per-seed relative change vs the reference, negative = better, bold = CI excludes 0. µs/step: CPU, batch 1.
