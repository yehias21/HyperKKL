# Literature check: learned KKL for non-autonomous systems (2026-10-26)
Read in full text (local pdftotext): Bernard&Andrieu TAC (HAL hal-02050020), 2501.11655, 2210.01476, 2602.24252, Peralez&Nadri L4DC24 (PMLR v242), 2212.00866, 2204.00318, 2603.20434.

- Bernard & Andrieu, IEEE TAC 64(1):270-281, 2019 (online 2018), DOI 10.1109/TAC.2018.2872202. PDE (3): dT/dx(x,t) f(x,u(t)) + dT/dt(x,t) = A T(x,t) + B h(x,u(t)); T implicitly depends on u (causal). T0(x,t)=int_0^t e^{A(t-s)} B Y(x,t;s;u) ds. Thm 4: control-affine, instantaneously uniformly observable + drift strongly diff. observable of order dx -> stationary T, observer xi' = kA xi + B y + phi(xi) u, phi = dT/dx g o T*. No learning.
- Niazi, Cao, Sun, Das, Johansson, ACC 2023 pp.3048-3055, arXiv 2210.01476. Autonomous. Loss (7) data + (8) PDE residual dT/dx f - A T - B h at "physics points" = odd samples of simulated trajectories (even samples for regression).
- Niazi, Cao, Barreau, Johansson, arXiv 2501.11655 (v2 Nov 2025). Autonomous only (non-autonomous explicitly out of scope; future work: "time-varying ... time-dependent PDEs"). Sequential: R1 = data + nu*PDE residual (12); PDE collocation S_pde = states along q simulated trajectories; then inverse.
- Peralez & Nadri, L4DC 2024, PMLR 242:929-940 (model-free, switching). Bioreactor with u: latent A learned at constant u0, then z+ = Az + By + Psi(z,u), Psi = T(f(T^-1 z,u)) - T(f(T^-1 z,u0)) (model required; from Peralez&Nadri CDC 2021 pp.4370-4375). No PDE loss.
- Miao & Gatsis, L4DC 2023, PMLR 211:208-219, arXiv 2212.00866. Neural-ODE, loss on state error; claims extension to nonautonomous via stationary T (B&A); tested learned autonomous observer under u=cos(12t). PDE constraint tried in appendix (|dT/dx f - (DT+Fy)|), dropped (RMSE 0.98 vs 0.86).
- Buisson-Fenet, Bahr, Morgenthaler, Di Meglio, IFAC-PapersOnLine 56(2):4061-4067, 2023, arXiv 2204.00318, DOI 10.1016/j.ifacol.2023.10.1730. Autonomous; supervised; optional T*(z, omega_c) conditioning on gain parameter; nonautonomous = future work.
- Woelk, Morris, Tang, arXiv 2602.24252 (v2 Apr 2026, "Neural Luenberger state observer for nonautonomous nonlinear systems", NLOX). Model-free; stationary T + input-affine term omega(z)u (B&A Thm 4), Euler-discretized; learns omega and T^dagger with loss ||x - T^dagger(z)||^2 over trajectories; no PDE loss.
- Berin-Costain, Wang, Morris, Liu, arXiv 2603.20434: PINN KKL error bounds, autonomous.
- No verified paper learns a time-varying T(x,t)/T(x,u-history) with a PDE loss containing dT/dt (besides the user's own HyperKKL arXiv 2602.22630 / 2603.29744).
